## The short version

Expense Tracker keeps your data **on your phone**. There is no account, no sign-up, no server and no tracking. We (the developer) never receive, see or store your data.

## What data the app handles

The app stores what you enter: transactions (amount, date, category, payment method, note), categories and budgets, recurring rules, and your settings (currency, theme). This data is stored **only on your device**, inside the app's private storage.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** The app has no account system, no analytics, no advertising, no crash-reporting and no third-party trackers. We do not run any server that your data could be sent to.

## When data leaves your phone

Only if **you** choose it, and only to a place **you** control:

- **Folder sync (optional).** If you pick a folder (for example in Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive or on your phone), the app saves one file, `expense-tracker-data.json`, in that folder. Whoever has access to that folder can read it. The app can only access the folder you pick.
- **Google Sheet (optional, advanced).** If you enter the address of your own Google Apps Script web app, the app sends your transactions to that address, which writes them to a Google Sheet that you own.
- **Backups and exports.** When you create a backup or export a CSV, the app hands the file to Android's share sheet. Where it goes next is your choice.

Once data is in a folder or sheet you chose, it is handled under the terms of that service (for example Google Drive's) — not by us.

## Permissions

The app does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or general storage. It can read and write a folder **only** if you choose one with Android's folder picker. It uses the vibration permission for tap feedback.

## Your choices and control

- **Export** your data any time (CSV or backup file) from Settings.
- **Stop syncing** any time in Settings → Sync & Backup. Files already in your folder or sheet are not deleted.
- **Delete everything** on your phone in Settings → Privacy & Data → Delete all my data. Uninstalling the app also removes its stored data.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.
